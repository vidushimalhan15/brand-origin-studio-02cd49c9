# 🚗 Driving Test — Study Log

My personal companion for passing the driving theory exam. Whenever I hit a
concept, sign, or question I don't understand, I drop it in here. Claude
explains it clearly, tracks it, and we revise the weak spots before the exam.

---

## How this works

1. **I drop in** anything I don't get — a question, a road sign, a rule, a term.
2. **Claude writes a clear explanation** and files it under a topic below.
3. Each entry gets a **confidence rating** so we know what still needs work:
   - 🔴 **Shaky** — just learned it, don't trust myself yet
   - 🟡 **Getting there** — mostly get it, needs another pass
   - 🟢 **Solid** — confident, revised and remembered
4. **We revise** the 🔴 and 🟡 entries using the Revision Queue at the bottom.

> Tip: just paste the question or concept in chat. You don't need to format
> anything — I'll slot it into the right place and update the tracker.
